# Alasia: Lost Isles

*A maritime dark-fantasy action RPG.*

## The Fourth Bell

Siv walks out onto the ferry plank you mended and gives a little bounce. The passengers laugh. Minutes later, she is down under the quay with her hand knotted in another worker's belt, holding his mouth above the water. Something in the wall keeps lifting his arm. Your stamp is on the order that let the sea in.

Across the Sound a grain ship is unloading in Verran. Its harbor stays level because the great jacks at Selwick drive the strain through the rock into Orren. Every sling the crew takes ashore costs the island beneath your feet. You have six high waters before the repair at South Cut gives way.

The last time a shore went under, Ada Sen was the girl at the ferry rail. People had started uphill. She called them back. A boat was coming, and she had a brass tally to prove it. On one exceptional ebb you can go down with her to the ruined Market Steps and put your hand in the socket where she left it.

Read [The Fourth Bell, the complete three-act campaign](docs/02-narrative/campaign-spine.md). The [scene scripts](docs/02-narrative/writing-and-quest-standards.md) and [principal cast](docs/02-narrative/cast-and-arcs.md) accompany the manuscript. Alasia is in narrative preproduction. An earlier short browser study follows Ada's crossing to Sere.

## Play Market Steps

On GitHub, choose **Code → Download ZIP**, extract it and open [studies/market-steps.html](studies/market-steps.html) in a browser. It runs offline. You arrive at an exceptional ebb with Ada Sen, who worked the landing when there was still a town here. Pick where the pilot should put the skiff, get Ada across the old market, and bring home one half of a passage tally. In Verran you can find out where the missing ferry went and what became of a family who rented a room behind the oven. Use the action buttons with a pointer or Tab and Enter.

For the opening on Orren, read [The long board](docs/03-gameplay/orren-field-sequence.md). For the working settlements across the Sound, read [Three landings in the Inner Sound](docs/01-world/island-life.md).

## Working approach

Write and build from the **seed outward**. Keep geography, history, social structure, enemy ecology and classes connected to things that actually happened in the world. Favor a smaller number of distinctive, fully realized places and mechanics over an ever-expanding inventory of names. Challenge tired language, derivative designs and easy plot devices by improving them in the work itself.

The first playable area is envisioned on **Orren**: Low Quay, Gauge Steps, Reedward and South Cut. It provides a concrete place to express the world and test the combat, without treating the rest of the narrative as a bureaucratic dependency.

The existing repository [LICENSE](LICENSE) is AGPL-3.0; music, art, fonts, commissioned work and other assets will need appropriate rights. No third-party game assets or UI designs are to be copied.

The Sere study uses an illustrated, turn-based chart. Orren’s combat, the three professions and the full Three.js campaign remain design work.
