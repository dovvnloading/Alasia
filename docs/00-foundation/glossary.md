# Working glossary and name sheet

**ID:** GOV-002 · **Status:** WORKING · **Version:** 0.1

All names here except **Alasia: Lost Isles** are provisional. One spelling per concept across narrative, UI, concept art and code. Prefer ordinary speech over ornamental fantasy epithets.

## World and physical terms

| Term | Meaning | Rule / misuse to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alasia** | The inhabited archipelago and the civic world of the game | Not a planet, dimension or lost empire |
| **Inner Sound** | The sheltered channels linking the standing islands | It is tidal and navigable on specific routes; not an infinite open ocean |
| **Seamglass** | Rare crystal seams that temporarily retain and route mechanical strain when properly fitted | It never conjures force or matter |
| **Sill** | A fixed pressure-transfer station with foundations, calibrated collars and tide-driven charge | It is infrastructure, not a shrine |
| **Sill network** | Connected exchange of lift and settlement across underlying plates | Recipients and donors are materially coupled |
| **Lift** | Local elevation gained by a receiving plate | Requires a source of work and settlement elsewhere |
| **Settlement** | Ground lost at a donor plate under sustained routing | Measurable; cannot be reversed for free |
| **Allocation** | A decision about which plate receives lift and which bears settlement | Not a generic magical spell |
| **The Third Column** | The suppressed donor-settlement field in the Board's original work registers | Its records are hidden by people, not erased by sorcery |
| **The Winter Allocation** | Decision to use inhabited Sere as donor, forty-one years before the campaign | A culpable choice made during a real emergency |
| **Anset Trials** | Public transfer trials at a shoal with no resident households | Harbor gain and loss of the boat crews' beach both entered the public account |
| **Held** | Injured workers whose damaged harnesses keep reapplying dangerous strain impulses | People first; not undead or inherently evil |
| **Bridleshells** | Vibration-sensitive coastal shell fauna displaced by subsoil failures | Wildlife; no magic soul-puppets |
| **Chalk colonies** | Calcium-forming fissure organisms amplified by repeated strain pulses | Ecological threat with constrained behavior |

## Naming constraints

No arbitrary apostrophes; no “Shadow/Death/Blood/Eternal + noun” conventions; no title that claims an ancient cosmic order when an ordinary trade, place or job can carry the history. New names must survive out-loud reading, uniqueness review, consistency with local speech, and an etymology/usage explanation. Preserve a map of alias names if historical records call a place something different.
