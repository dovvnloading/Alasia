    Controls and profession technique

    Move with WASD or arrows, or click the ground. Click an enemy to close and strike. Hold Shift and click to strike in place. Right-click releases your signature. J, E and Q work with keyboard aim. Space evades. R works the marked haul point. On touch, drag the movement pad and hold an action.

    With a standard controller, move with the left stick, aim with the right, strike with the right trigger, set your tool with X, release with Y, evade with A, and work with B. Start pauses.
